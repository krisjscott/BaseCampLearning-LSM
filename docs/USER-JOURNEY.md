# Learner Journey

## 1. Access and authentication

1. Learner opens Base Camp from Ties HQ or a direct link.
2. Learner chooses **Sign in with email** or **Sign in with Crew ID**.
3. The system verifies the account, status, organization, and audience permissions.
4. Unauthorized, expired, or unknown accounts receive a recovery action and support route.

## 2. First-time onboarding

1. Welcome and short explanation of Base Camp.
2. Confirm name, profile, department, role, and audience class.
3. Select current skills, experience level, and learning goals.
4. Set a realistic weekly learning commitment.
5. Take a short platform tour.
6. Land on assigned, mandatory, and recommended learning.

Returning learners skip onboarding and go to Learning Home.

## 3. Learning Home

Learning Home prioritizes action:

- Continue the current course
- Mandatory learning and deadlines
- Upcoming checkpoints
- Assigned courses
- Recommended paths
- Recent progress and completed credentials
- Current XP, level, streak, and next milestone

## 4. Discover and evaluate

Learners can browse and search by skill, function, level, duration, format, instructor, and certificate availability. The course detail page shows outcomes, syllabus, prerequisites, workload, assessment rules, instructor, reviews, and a certificate preview.

## 5. Enroll or accept assignment

- Optional courses can be enrolled in directly.
- Mandatory courses clearly show the assigner, due date, and consequence of non-completion.
- Prerequisites are checked before access.
- The learner sees an estimated completion plan and can add checkpoints to a calendar when supported.

## 6. Course orientation

Before the first lesson, the learner sees the course overview, module sequence, grading model, completion rules, deadlines, available resources, accessibility settings, and support route.

## 7. Lesson player

The lesson player keeps the current activity central while showing the course outline and progress.

For a required non-skippable video:

1. The learner watches from the recorded position.
2. Seeking beyond the furthest verified watch point is blocked.
3. Playback progress is periodically saved.
4. Background-tab, connectivity, and device changes are handled without losing valid progress.
5. The lesson completes only when the configured watch threshold is met.
6. The compulsory quiz is then unlocked.

## 8. Compulsory quiz

1. Quiz instructions show question count, time limit, pass mark, and attempt policy.
2. Answers autosave where appropriate.
3. Submission produces the configured feedback.
4. A pass unlocks the next required activity.
5. A fail shows what can be reviewed and when another attempt is allowed.
6. Every attempt is dated and retained for authorized reporting.

## 9. Readings, resources, and assignments

Readings can require confirmation or a knowledge check. Resources can be viewed or downloaded according to policy. Assignments support instructions, rubrics, files or links, draft saving, submission receipts, review status, scores, and feedback.

## 10. Dated progress

The progress view is both a percentage and an evidence trail. It records:

- Course assigned or enrolled
- Course started
- Lesson and module completion
- Verified watch completion
- Quiz attempts and pass dates
- Assignment submission and review
- Deadline or schedule changes
- Final assessment
- Course completion
- Certificate issue, expiry, renewal, or revocation

Each event shows its date, status, and related information.

## 11. Notifications

Notifications cover assignments, upcoming deadlines, overdue work, unlocked activities, feedback, completion, and certificates. Delivery may include in-app, email, and Ties HQ. Mandatory compliance alerts cannot be fully disabled.

## 12. Final assessment and completion

The system checks that every required lesson, watch threshold, quiz, assignment, and final assessment condition has been satisfied. When complete, it records the final score, skills, time spent, and completion date, awards valid XP, and starts certificate generation.

## 13. Certificate and accomplishments

The verified certificate is added to accomplishments. The learner can download a PDF, copy a verification link, share the credential, and see any expiry or renewal requirement.

## 14. Growth loop

After completion the learner can add the skill to their profile, rate the course, submit feedback, join a learning path, and start a recommended next-level or related course.

## 15. Help and exceptions

Support is available from every stage for login, accessibility, playback, progress, quiz, deadline, submission, content, or certificate issues. Recovery messaging must say whether work was saved and return the learner to the interrupted activity after resolution.
